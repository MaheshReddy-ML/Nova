# offers

One sentence: NOVA can diagnose which of the four value-equation levers is broken, audit a six-component offer anatomy, and design honest bonuses, guarantees, and scarcity that raise perceived value without manipulation.

## When to use

- User says "my offer isn't converting" or "I want to improve my offer"
- Designing or redesigning a service, course, coaching, or high-ticket B2B offer
- Adding bonuses, structuring guarantees, or creating real scarcity
- User asks why an offer isn't selling or how to add urgency honestly

## Workflow (numbered steps adapted to NOVA's tools)

1. **Identify business type** — ask whether the offer is for services, courses, coaching, info products, SaaS, or high-ticket B2B. The playbook is type-specific.
2. **State the current offer in plain language** — prompt the user to name, price, describe what they get, list the guarantee, and note any deadline. Write it down.
3. **Run the value equation** — score each of the four levers (Dream outcome, Perceived likelihood, Time delay, Effort & sacrifice) 1–10. The lowest score is the binding constraint.
4. **Audit the anatomy** — check which of the six components (Core deliverable, Bonus stack, Guarantee, Scarcity/urgency, Name, Price + payment structure) is missing or weak.
5. **Pick one lever to fix this iteration** — do not rebuild everything. The biggest lever is usually the one scoring lowest.
6. **Draft the changed component** — new bonus, new guarantee, new scarcity, new name, or new payment plan. Use the reference library for format-specific playbooks.
7. **Project the lift, honestly** — plan on a 10–40% relative lift from one component change. Multiply lifts, do not add them: +20% then +30% = 1.20 × 1.30 = 1.56 (2% → 3.12%, +1.12 percentage points). Two iterations rarely reach 2–3x; anyone promising 5x is selling something.

## Quality bar (checklist)

- [ ] Business type identified and playbook selected
- [ ] Current offer stated in plain language (name, price, deliverable, guarantee, deadline)
- [ ] Value-equation levers scored 1–10; lowest lever flagged as constraint
- [ ] Anatomy audit: all six components present and at least three rated strong
- [ ] Only one component changed per iteration; lift projected with multiplication, not addition
- [ ] No manipulative scarcity, over-promising guarantees, bonus inflation, or course-bro aesthetics
- [ ] Specific numbers, named customers, concrete outcomes, and real timelines used instead of superlatives

## Pitfalls

- **Manipulative scarcity** — fake countdown timers or "only 3 spots left" lies destroy trust long-term.
- **Over-promising guarantees** — "double your revenue or refund + $1,000" eats margin and risks public reputation failure.
- **Bonus inflation** — stacking $50K of bonuses on a $497 product so it "feels like a steal" is seen through by sophisticated buyers.
- **Course-bro aesthetic** — gold logos, "secret method," fake urgency pattern-matches to scam; wrong room for serious products.
- **Discounting to acquire** — discount-askers churn at ~2× the rate of full-price customers; a coupon anchors the product as cheap. Discount only for upgrades/cross-sells or real seasonal windows. Raise value with an offer instead.
