# Social Content

One sentence: NOVA helps you create platform-appropriate organic social content—posts, threads, carousels, and short-form video scripts—grounded in storytelling, audience alignment, and engagement mechanics, without AI tells or broetry.

## When to use

- User asks what to post on LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook, or which format to favor.
- User wants to build a content calendar, repurpose existing material, or reverse-engineer viral patterns in their niche.
- User needs guidance on content pillars, hook formulas, or engagement routines for community growth.

## Workflow (numbered steps adapted to NOVA's tools)

1. **Identify platform and goal** — ask which platform(s), primary objective (brand awareness, leads, traffic, community), and desired action.
2. **Gather context** — query user on audience, brand voice, resources, and existing content. If `.agents/brand.md` or `.agents/product-marketing.md` exists, read it first.
3. **Select content pillars** — choose 3–5 pillars aligning expertise and audience interests; use the Pillars Framework table as a template.
4. **Choose a hook formula** — match hook type (curiosity, story, value, contrarian) to the post goal; avoid AI tells, broetry, and manufactured vulnerability.
5. **Adapt or extract content** — if repurposing, pull content atoms (quotable moments, story arcs, tactical tips) from blogs, podcasts, or webinars; adapt format, length, and tone per platform.
6. **Draft post copy** — write standalone captions using hook formulas and platform-specific limits; never use stock phrases, emoji bullets, or contrast-reveal structures.
7. **Review quality bar** — checklist: story-first, no AI tells, platform format respected, hook in first line, one-liner lists, no emoji bullets.
8. **Schedule or save** — if user has a scheduling tool with API/MCP, create drafts for approval; otherwise, output copy and calendar for user execution.

## Quality bar (checklist)

- [ ] Story-first: every recommendation centers on the user's narrative, not algorithm hacks.
- [ ] No AI tells: no "It's not X, it's Y" contrasts, no negation lists, no trailing pile-ons, no stock phrases ("Say goodbye to," "Unlock the power of").
- [ ] No broetry: no one-sentence-per-line formatting; paragraphs break where a person would pause.
- [ ] Platform format respected: carousel slide count, thread length, video duration, character limits observed.
- [ ] Hook present in first line: curiosity, story, value, or contrarian; specific fact, number, or story included.
- [ ] One-liner lists: lists of three max; no emoji bullets.
- [ ] Standalone captions: each post works without context from previous posts.

## Pitfalls

- Slow video hooks; viewers scroll past if the first 3 seconds don't combine visual, verbal, and text overlay.
- Posting external links in post body on LinkedIn/X; reach declines algorithmically.
- Ignoring brand voice or audience data; content feels generic and underperforms.
- Over-promotional posts (>10% of content); audience disengages.
- No CTA or next step; audience knows what to do after engaging.
- Skipping engagement routine; algorithm favors accounts that interact with others.
